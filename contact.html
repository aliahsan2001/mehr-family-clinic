Contact & directions

Finding us
is easy.

Ground floor, step-free entrance, parking right outside. Here's how to get to us.

Directions

  1. By car

    From University Road, turn into Block 7 at the petrol station. We're on the right after the park, with free parking out front.

  2. By bus or rickshaw

    Get off at the Block 7 stop on University Road. Walk east past the petrol station, then turn right at the corner. It takes about five minutes.

  3. Easy access

    Everything is on the ground floor, with a ramp at the entrance and seating near the door. Ask reception if you need a wheelchair.
